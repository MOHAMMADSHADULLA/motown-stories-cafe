Our Story

A Garage. A Dream.
A Café.

Motown Stories Café is Hyderabad's most unique automotive-themed dining experience, nestled in the heart of Financial District, Gachibowli. Step into a world of vintage motorcycles, rustic bikes, bold graffiti murals, and warm Edison bulbs — where petrolheads and food lovers come together.

  • 🏍️ Vintage cars & motorcycles on display
  • ☕ Specialty coffee & handcrafted drinks
  • 🎨 Graffiti murals & garage art throughout
  • 🅿️ Ample free parking
  • 🌙 Open daily till midnight
